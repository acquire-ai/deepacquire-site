Hi everyone, I’m a language learner and software engineer.

I’ve spent the last 9 months heads-down building this AI-native language learning tool — **[DeepAcquire](https://deepacquire.com/)**. Like many of you, I used to be a heavy user of ***Language Reactor*** and ***eJoy***. While they are great tools, I felt something was missing. They excel at giving you subtitles and machine translations, but in the age of AI, we can do so much more.

I realized we should leverage AI's true strength: the ability to provide **explanations and teaching based on context and your personal situation**. True language acquisition happens when you understand the *nuance* and *context*, not just the dictionary definition.

So, I decided to build this tool from scratch with an **AI-First mindset**.

## The Problem with Traditional Tools

Most existing subtitle tools act as a layer on top of video players. They are excellent at:

* Displaying dual subtitles.
* Providing instant dictionary lookups.
* Saving words to a flashcard deck.

However, they often lack **Context**. When you look up a word, you get a generic definition. When you review it later, you might forget *why* it was used in that specific scene.

## What Makes DeepAcquire Different?

**DeepAcquire** is designed to be the **"Cursor for Language Learning"**. Just like Cursor changed how we code by integrating AI deeply into the workflow, DeepAcquire changes how we learn languages by integrating AI into the consumption of content.

### 1. AI-Refined Subtitles

We don't just display what YouTube gives us. We use AI to improve raw auto-generated captions. DeepAcquire corrects punctuation, fixes sentence breaks, and provides a much cleaner, more readable subtitle experience than the default ones.

### 2. Adaptive Explanations

This is where the "AI-First" approach shines. The AI explains vocabulary based on the **specific video context** and **your current proficiency level**.

* **For Beginners:** It uses simple words to explain concepts.
* **For Advanced Learners:** It provides nuance and examples relevant to your daily life.

### 3. Deep Dives & Q&A

You can literally chat with the video. Confused by two similar-looking words?

* *Ask the AI:* "Are these words etymologically related?"
* *Ask the AI:* "How do I distinguish them in this context?"

You get precise, context-aware answers instantly, without leaving the immersion of the video.

### 4. Contextual Recall

When reviewing words, we don't just show "Word -> Native Language." We save the **exact scene and context** where you first encountered the word. This helps you recall the "moment," making the memory stickier than a mechanical definition.

## The Vision: Beyond YouTube

I am currently developing a **Mobile App** that expands this philosophy beyond just video:

* **Learn from Anything:** Import materials from your daily work and life (blogs, docs, articles).
* **Smart Audio:** The AI will not only explain texts but also **read them aloud** to you in a natural voice.
* **Long-term AI Memory:** The AI will get to know you better over time, personalizing every example based on your learning history.
